THE PRINCIPAL'S CODE NOTE

“The numbers must be gathered in this order. Do not follow the order in which you found them.”

  1. Begin with the mark hidden in the second book in the Library: three times three.
  2. Next, take the mark from the place where students sleep: ten divided by two.
  3. Then return to the first book in the Library: four minus three.
  4. After that, visit the place of experiments: eight minus five.
  5. Next comes the room where lecturers prepare their work: three plus four.
  6. Finish in the classroom. The code ends with nothing.

Match each answer to the fragment from that location. The final line tells you the last digit.
